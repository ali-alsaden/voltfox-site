Astra Blocks Out now

Save planets one block at a time. A calm, one-thumb space puzzle.

Drag pieces onto the grid. Fill a row or column and it clears. Every level is a planet under threat: clear it and watch it come back to life.

  • Campaign: hand-built levels across a star map, with no clock and no pressure.
  • Deep Space: an endless mode against the clock. Every cleared line adds seconds.
  • Power-ups: Forge, Rotate, Invert and Laser for when nothing fits.
  • Fair by design: every hand-built level is checked so it can always be solved.
  • Something new every day: a daily gift, chests, ranks and unlockable themes.

Free with optional ads and purchases. No subscription, ever. No account needed.
