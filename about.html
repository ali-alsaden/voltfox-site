About us

Our mission

Little games worth coming back to for years.

Who we are

Voltfox Games is a small independent studio in Michigan. We make free games for phones, starting with Android.

Every game starts as one idea you can explain in a sentence: fit the pieces, pop the rings, guess the word. Then we polish it until it feels good in your hands, and we keep playing it long after launch.

How we make games

Quick to start

Open it, play a round, put it down. Most rounds fit in a coffee break.

Fair by design

Our hand-built levels are checked so they can always be solved. Purchases are optional and never needed to finish a level.

Free, no tricks

Free to play, with no subscriptions, ever. Ads stay out of your way, and one purchase removes ad breaks.

Your privacy

No account or login needed, and your progress is saved on your device. Details in our privacy policy.

Only the fun ones

We play every game before it ships. If an idea isn't fun, it doesn't ship.

Built to last

We keep adding levels, themes and daily rewards, so there's always a reason to come back.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: support@voltfoxgames.com. We read everything.
