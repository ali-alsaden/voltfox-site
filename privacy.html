Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Voltfox Games LLC ("we", "us") makes mobile games, including Astra Blocks, Tangent, Bubble Vortex, and Fox Fight, and any other game we publish that links to this policy (each a "Game"). This policy explains what information the Games collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. Use of the Games is also governed by our Terms of Service.

Information we collect

We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or contacts.

The Games automatically collect, through the third-party services below:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (e.g. Google Advertising ID, the Firebase app instance ID, and an anonymous app user ID created by RevenueCat)
  • Gameplay and usage events (e.g. sessions, levels played, scores, ads viewed, purchases made) — not linked to your name or contact details
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (country/region level, used for ad delivery and legal compliance)
  • Crash and diagnostic data
  • Purchase information (product purchased, receipt token). Payment is processed entirely by Google Play billing; we never see your payment details.

Saved progress

Your game progress (for example levels, coins, gems, which purchases you own, and settings) is saved on your device only. The Games do not currently upload your progress to our servers, to Firebase, or to any other cloud service, so we cannot restore it for you. If you uninstall a Game, clear its data, or change devices, your progress and unspent coins and gems may be lost. The ad-free unlock and bundles that include it can be restored with the Restore Purchases button (see below). If we add cloud save in a future update, we will update this policy before it goes live.

How we use information

  • Operate the Games and save your progress on your device
  • Show ads (interstitials, for example between levels, and optional rewarded ads, for example a second chance or bonus coins in exchange for watching), including personalized ads where permitted
  • Process, validate, and restore in-app purchases
  • Fix crashes, balance difficulty, and improve the Games
  • Comply with legal obligations

Legal bases (EEA/UK): performance of contract (running the Games), consent (personalized advertising), and legitimate interests (analytics, security).

Your choices

  • Android: you can delete or reset your Advertising ID in system Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before personalized ads are shown, you'll see a consent prompt from Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's Settings screen. Where similar laws apply elsewhere, we follow them.
  • Purchases: the Games have no subscriptions. The one-time ad-free unlock, coin packs, gem packs, and bundles are single purchases managed through your Google Play account. The ad-free unlock removes interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads stay available. A Restore Purchases button in each Game re-applies the ad-free unlock (and bundles that include it) on a new or reset device signed in to the same Google Play account. Coins, gems, and other consumable items are used up when spent and cannot be restored.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including GDPR and US state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete, or correct personal information, and to opt out of "sale"/"sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising (which the ad services above may constitute). To exercise any right, contact us at support@voltfoxgames.com. We do not knowingly discriminate for exercising privacy rights.

Children

The Games are intended for players aged 13 and over and are not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The Games are not part of Google Play's Families program. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention & security

Analytics data is retained per the providers' standard retention periods (Firebase: up to 14 months for user-level data as configured). Saved progress stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. We use reputable providers with industry-standard security. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the date above and, where appropriate, in-app notice.
