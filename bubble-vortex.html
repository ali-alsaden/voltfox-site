How a level plays

First, aim from the core.

Five power‑ups

Two you take into a level, three you use during one. Each is offered only on the levels where it earns its price.

  • Freeze

    Before a level. The siege starts frozen.

  • Photon Beam

    Before a level. Stays on all level: every 10th shot is a beam that burns a straight line through the siege.

  • Bomb

    During a level. Your next shot pops everything around it.

  • Prism

    During a level. Your next shot matches any colour.

  • Core Blast

    During a level. Clears the inner layer of the siege.

Questions

When can I play it?

Bubble Vortex is in testing now. It comes to Google Play first. Leave your address below and the studio writes once, on the day it is out.

Which phones does it run on?

Android phones running Android 8.0 or newer. It is made for one thumb in portrait.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at launch. Android comes first.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It is free, with short ad breaks between levels. A one-time $2.99 purchase removes them, and coin and gem packs are optional. There are no subscriptions.

Can I play if I am colour-blind?

Yes. Every colour also carries its own shape: circle, plus, square, diamond, triangle and star.

How many levels are there?

Thirty designed levels across three worlds, then Deep Space: endless boards that get a little harder each lap.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login, no chat and no location permission. Your progress is saved on your phone.

Who makes it?

Voltfox Games, a small independent studio in Michigan. Write to support@voltfoxgames.com with anything at all.
